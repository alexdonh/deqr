Image access

deQR reads QR codes out of the page itself and needs no special access for images hosted by the site you are visiting. Images served from another origin without CORS headers cannot be read at all - the browser blocks it. Add those origins here to let deQR fetch them directly. Nothing is sent anywhere; the fetch happens locally in your browser.
